App name: Inflamend

Description:

Inflamend helps people living with ulcerative colitis, Crohn's disease, and IBD track daily symptoms, bowel movements, meals, medications, sleep, weight, notes, and possible flare patterns. It is designed for quick logging on hard days and clear summaries for GI appointments.

Inflamend does not diagnose, treat, prescribe, or replace professional medical care. Serious or rapidly worsening symptoms require medical attention.
