# Inflamend Terms of Use Draft

Draft last updated: June 2, 2026.

Status: draft for product, legal, privacy, and security review. This document is not a published agreement and must be reviewed by counsel before App Store submission or public use.

## Acceptance

These draft terms describe expected conditions for using Inflamend. A final version must be published with company identity, contact details, jurisdiction terms, and support/deletion workflows before launch.

## Purpose of Inflamend

Inflamend is intended to help users track self-reported IBD-related information and prepare for conversations with clinicians. Users can log symptoms, bowel movements, meals, medication status, sleep, weight, notes, Care messages, and reports.

Inflamend is not a medical device, emergency service, diagnosis tool, treatment plan, prescription service, or substitute for professional medical care.

## Medical and Emergency Disclaimer

Users should not rely on Inflamend to diagnose a condition, triage an emergency, decide whether to start, stop, or change medication, or decide whether urgent care is needed.

For severe, rapidly worsening, or unsafe symptoms, users should contact a clinician, urgent care, emergency services, or local crisis resources as appropriate.

## User Responsibilities

Users are responsible for:

- Entering accurate information when they choose to log data.
- Reviewing any generated report before sharing it.
- Discussing medical questions with a qualified clinician.
- Protecting their device, account access, and exported files.
- Avoiding entry of another person's health information unless authorized.

## Accounts and Backend Status

The current app contains a local account scaffold for flow testing. Production authentication, Keychain-backed tokens, backend profile sync, backend deletion, and cloud export receipts are required before release.

If production accounts are enabled, final terms must describe account creation, account deletion, support contact, suspension, and data handling workflows.

## User Content

Users keep responsibility for health logs, notes, transcripts, messages, and exports they create. Inflamend may process user content to provide tracking, summaries, reports, local safety framing, and user-requested app features.

Production use of cloud storage, AI services, analytics, or diagnostics must follow the final privacy policy.

## Care Assistant and Education Content

Care assistant responses, education guides, insights, risk summaries, and reports are informational. They may be incomplete or incorrect and must be reviewed with a clinician before medical decisions are made.

Inflamend should refuse prescription-change instructions and should show safety framing for red-flag symptoms, but it cannot guarantee detection of every urgent situation.

## Exports and Sharing

Doctor reports and user-data exports may contain sensitive health information. Users should review exports before sharing and should share them only with trusted recipients.

Cloud export jobs and receipts are planned but not implemented in this checkpoint.

## Payments and Subscriptions

No subscription, paid feature, or StoreKit flow is implemented in this checkpoint. If payments are added, final terms must describe billing, cancellation, refunds, trials, and App Store purchase rules.

## Intellectual Property

The app name, interface, source content, and generated assets belong to the project owner or applicable licensors. Users should not copy, modify, reverse engineer, or misuse the app except as allowed by applicable law and open-source license terms if any are published.

## Availability and Changes

Inflamend may change, pause, or discontinue features. Final launch terms should explain how users are notified about material changes.

## Limitations

Final terms must include jurisdiction-appropriate warranty disclaimers, limitation of liability, dispute-resolution terms, governing law, and consumer-rights language. These legal sections require counsel review and are intentionally not finalized in this draft.
